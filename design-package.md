# OUVRAGE — Design Package (Tier 1)

## Brand premise
Maison Ouvrage. An invented French leather house. Quiet old-money luxury: no logos, no noise, proof of craft. The footer discloses the brand is fictional. Voice: calm, assured, short sentences, first person plural of a house that has nothing to prove.

## The one call to action
"Request a private appointment." Every section funnels to it. Form is a JS-only success state (demo brand, submissions go nowhere; success message says the atelier will reply, footer discloses fiction).

## Customer language (from research)
- Desires: "built to last decades", "stitched by hand", "one craftsman, one bag", "works everywhere without shouting", "cherished for years".
- Pains: "luxury prices for non-luxurious materials", straps peeling after one wear, stitching that does not match the price, logo-heavy regret.
- Objection: "am I paying for the ads or for the bag?" → answered in the Materials/Price section.

## Hero film (Tier 1, one 6s shot, 1080p 16:9)
The Atelier: slow dolly through a dim leather workshop at dusk. Foreground: hides, brass hardware glinting, workbench with tools. Camera glides forward and right, comes to rest on a single finished handbag (structured top-handle, deep espresso leather, small brass clasp, NO logos, NO text) on a stone pedestal under one warm shaft of light. Ending frame truly rests: bag right-of-center, left third in soft shadow = text lane.

## Palette (from the footage's world)
- --ink: #1a140f (espresso near-black, page ground; never pure black)
- --hide: #3d2b1c (deep saddle brown)
- --cognac: #a9713a (leather midtone)
- --brass: #c9a25e (accent, rare doses only)
- --bone: #ece4d6 (paper/cream text)

## Type trio
- Display: Cormorant Garamond 500/600 (French old-style serif)
- Body: Alegreya Sans (quiet humanist sans, warm at small sizes)
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono for small labels (N° 01, ATELIER, PARIS)

## Signature element
A single hand-drawn saddle-stitch line (SVG dashed path) that draws itself on scroll, running vertically through the page sections, stitching the story together. Whisper-level dust-mote particles in hero and CTA only. Soft brass glow on key display lines.

## Scroll journey beat map (bands over the video, left text lane)
1. 0–8%: OUVRAGE wordmark + "Maison de maroquinerie" small mono label. (scrim: soft left gradient)
2. 18–30%: "One craftsman. One bag. Two hundred hours."
3. 40–52%: "No logos. The leather is the signature."
4. 62–74%: "Brass, vachetta, linen thread. Nothing else."
5. 86–100%: settle: headline "Carried for life." + CTA button "Request a private appointment" beside the resting bag.

Bands are starting points; validated by flick test.

## Below the fold (sections)
1. **The House** — short manifesto. Copy: "We are a small house. We make few bags, slowly. Each one is cut, stitched and finished by a single craftsman, and signed inside with his initials. Not ours. His."
2. **The Craft** (3 steps, equal treatment, one still each):
   - 01 Cut — "Every hide is full-grain vachetta, cut by hand around the scars. We waste more leather than most houses buy."
   - 02 Stitch — "Saddle-stitched with waxed linen thread. Two needles, one seam, two hundred hours. A machine cannot tie this knot."
   - 03 Finish — "Edges are burnished with beeswax until they shine like the hardware. Then it rests for seven days before it leaves."
3. **The Bag** — ending frame reused as design image + honest pricing block. "The Ouvrage N°1 — 4,800 €. You are not paying for advertising. We do not advertise. You are paying for the hide, the hours, and the hands." Materials list in mono.
4. **Answers** (FAQ, the real objections):
   - Why no logo? "A logo tells strangers what you paid. The stitching tells them what you value."
   - Will it last? "The saddle stitch fails one hole at a time, never all at once. Bring it back in twenty years and we will make it new."
   - Why the wait? "We make eight bags a month. The waitlist is the price of that."
5. **Appointment** (CTA + form: name, email, message → JS success state: "Thank you. The atelier will write to you within two days.")
6. **Footer** — nav, "OUVRAGE is a fictional brand. Imagery is AI generated." + mono colophon.

## Interactive moment
In The Bag section: a slow drag/hover slider "turn the bag in the light" — actually a subtle brass-glow spotlight that follows the pointer over the ending-frame image. Reduced motion honored.
