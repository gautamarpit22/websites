# GIGGLEGRAIN — Design Package (Tier 1, shopping site)

## Brand premise
GiggleGrain. Invented Indian kids-food brand: traditional grains (ragi, millets, jowar) made fun for kids and honest for parents. Playful + wholesome. Footer discloses fiction. Voice: a mom who reads labels, talking to another mom. Short, warm, zero jargon.

## The one call to action
Add to cart → Order on WhatsApp. (Placeholder number +910000000000 until user provides.)

## Customer language (research)
- Not-in-it: "zero maida", "zero refined sugar", "no maltodextrin", "no preservatives"
- Growth: "ragi has more calcium than milk", "iron for brain development", "slow-release energy, no sugar crash"
- Fear: "will my picky eater actually eat it?"
- Trust: founded-by-mothers energy, traditional grains revived.

## Budget: 150 credits hard cap. Ask user above that. Track every spend.
Projected: frame 2 + video (12-54) + 8 category tiles 16 + 6 product shots 12 = 84-92.

## Hero film (6s, 16:9, 1080p): Grain to Giggle
Macro: golden ragi/millet grains cascading in slow motion in warm morning light → grains swirl into a soft flour cloud over a griddle, batter glimpse → settles on one smiley-face millet cookie on a cream plate, scattered grains around. Ending frame = adorable smiley cookie = mobile poster.
Start frame: macro cascade of golden millet grains mid-air over rustic wooden table, warm sunlight, cream ceramic bowl below, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no hands, no people.
