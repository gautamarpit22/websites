N° 04 · Answers

The questions people actually ask.

Why is there no logo?

A logo tells strangers what you paid. The stitching tells them what you value. The people who know, know.

Will it really last?

A saddle stitch fails one hole at a time, never all at once. Bring the bag back in twenty years and we will make it new. That promise is part of the price.

Why the wait?

We make eight bags a month. One craftsman starts your bag and the same craftsman finishes it. The waitlist is the price of that, and we will not shorten it by hurrying.

What am I actually paying for?

The hide, the hours, and the hands. Nothing in the price pays for advertising, because there is none.

Can I buy one outright, or must I commission it?

Both. Each month's eight bags are sold as made, first come, first served. A commission takes longer and is yours alone. The price is the same unless your ideas are expensive.
