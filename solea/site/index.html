Questions

Before you ask

The things every woman asks us first.

Are the heels actually comfortable?

Yes, and we mean it measurably. The cushioned insole and a balanced heel pitch mean your weight sits back on the heel bone, not crushed into your toes. All-day wear is the design brief, not a marketing line.

What if the size is wrong?

We collect and exchange free within 7 days. Order two sizes if you're between them and send one back. No forms, no fees, just message us on WhatsApp.

How do I order?

Pick your size, add to bag, and checkout sends your order to us on WhatsApp. We confirm within the hour and deliver in 3 to 5 days across India.

Do you restock sold-out pairs?

Small batches mean pairs do sell out. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reserve you one from the next batch.
