Questions

Asked before every first visit

The honest answers, before you even sit down.

Will it hurt?

Most treatments feel like warmth or light snapping, and numbing cream handles the rest. You'll know exactly what a session feels like before you commit to anything, because we'll tell you plainly.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your skin, and anyone who promises a number before seeing you is guessing. After your free consultation you'll get a written plan with the expected range and the honest cost of the whole course.

Is it safe for Indian skin?

Yes, when the right technology is used at the right settings. Indian skin is more prone to post-treatment pigmentation, so we use protocols and lasers chosen specifically for deeper skin tones. This is Dr. Kapoor's specialty.

Will I be pushed to buy a package?

No. The consultation is free, the plan is written, the prices are on this page, and the decision is yours to take home. If your skin needs nothing, we will tell you that too.
